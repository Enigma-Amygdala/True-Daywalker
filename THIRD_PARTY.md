## MinHook

The original 0.3.0 first-party source references MinHook as an external native-hooking dependency. The original dependency checkout was not preserved in the published historical snapshot. [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) documents a pinned upstream MinHook v1.3.4 dependency **for review builds**, not an assertion that this was the original historical dependency revision.
